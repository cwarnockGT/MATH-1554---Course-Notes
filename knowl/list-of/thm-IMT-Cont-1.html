Theorem 2.60 The Invertible Matrix Theorem (Continued).

Let \(A\) be an \(n\times n\) matrix. The following are equivalent:
🔗
  • (a)   \(A\) is invertible.
    🔗
  • (m)   The columns of \(A\) form a basis for \(\mathbb{R}^n\text{.}\)
    🔗
  • (n)   \(\operatorname{Col}A=\mathbb{R}^n\text{.}\)
    🔗
  • (o)   \(\operatorname{dim}\operatorname{Col}A=n\text{.}\)
    🔗
  • (p)   \(\operatorname{rank}A=n\text{.}\)
    🔗
  • (q)   \(\operatorname{Nul}A=\{\vec 0\}\text{.}\)
    🔗
  • (r)   \(\operatorname{dim}\operatorname{Nul}A=0\text{.}\)
    🔗
