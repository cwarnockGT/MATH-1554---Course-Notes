Theorem 6.3 Basic Identities of the Dot Product.

Let \(\vec u,\vec v,\vec w\in\mathbb{R}^n\) and \(c\in\mathbb{R}\text{.}\)
🔗
  1. (Symmetry) \(\vec u\cdot\vec w = \vec w\cdot\vec u\text{.}\)
    🔗
  2. (Linearity) \((\vec v+\vec w)\cdot\vec u = \vec v\cdot\vec u + \vec w\cdot\vec u\text{.}\)
    🔗
  3. (Scalars) \((c\vec u)\cdot\vec w = c(\vec u\cdot\vec w) = \vec u \cdot (c\vec w)\text{.}\)
    🔗
  4. (Positivity) \(\vec u\cdot\vec u\ge 0\text{,}\) and it equals 0 if and only if \(\vec u=\vec 0\text{.}\)
    🔗
