Theorem 7.33 Extrema via Eigenvalues.

Let \(Q(\vec x)=\vec x^{\,T}A\vec x\text{,}\) where \(A\) is a real symmetric \(n\times n\) matrix with eigenvalues
🔗
\begin{equation*} \lambda_1 \ge \lambda_2 \ge \cdots \ge \lambda_n \end{equation*}
and corresponding normalized eigenvectors
🔗
\begin{equation*} \vec u_1,\vec u_2,\ldots,\vec u_n. \end{equation*}
Under the constraint \(\|\vec x\|=1\text{:}\)
🔗
  • The maximum value of \(Q\) is \(\lambda_1\text{,}\) attained at \(\vec x=\pm\vec u_1\text{.}\)
    🔗
  • The minimum value of \(Q\) is \(\lambda_n\text{,}\) attained at \(\vec x=\pm\vec u_n\text{.}\)
    🔗
